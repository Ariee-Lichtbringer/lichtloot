GuildLoot Classic Era profession database

Recipe identifiers, profession assignments, skill requirements and reagent quantities
were extracted from the Classic table and Classic crafting listings in AtlasLootClassic.
Source: https://github.com/Hoizame/AtlasLootClassic
Files: AtlasLootClassic/Data/Profession.lua and AtlasLootClassic_Crafting/data.lua
Retrieved: 2026-09-10
License of source data: GPL-2.0; see PROFESSION-DATA-LICENSE.txt.

Only the Classic Era recipe listing is included (1,246 unique recipes).
No TBC, Wrath or Season of Discovery recipe tables are included.
Localized spell/item names and icons are resolved by the WoW client.
Herbalism, Skinning and Fishing have no crafting recipes.
